# Initial Product Quarry

These are candidate commercial proofs, not shop-ready products.

## 1. Evidence Dock

Archive source: 2026-09-13 Motion, Reset and May Continuation.

Purpose: repeatable phone/camera positioning and visible scale/reference for comparable experiment recordings.

Why attractive:
- low technical risk;
- immediately useful to the Observatory itself;
- small print;
- clear measurable benefit: repositioning error / stability;
- can become a workshop accessory before becoming a consumer product.

Missing before CAD:
- actual phone + case dimensions and mass;
- preferred view;
- mounting surface / clamp;
- experiment footprint;
- material and hardware.

## 2. Motion Cell — accessible compliant-joint analogue

Archive source: 2026-09-13 Motion, Reset and May Continuation.

First test:
- one compliant joint;
- small thickness/geometry variant set;
- guarded travel;
- measure displacement/angle, return offset, damage, recovery/drift.

Commercial potential depends on a real repeatable effect and rights-safe original geometry.

## 3. Small resonance demonstrator

Existing build programme identifies the Programmable Resonance & Collision Bench as a major physical priority.

Potential small product descendant:
- one visible adjustable resonator;
- simple excitation;
- obvious change in response;
- measured frequency / decay;
- printable geometry plus standard hardware.

Requires a specific bounded design before promotion.

## Current recommendation

Use **Evidence Dock** as the first low-risk commercial proof accessory while **Verified Physics Print #001** remains a phenomenon-first product requiring a successful physical mechanism test.
