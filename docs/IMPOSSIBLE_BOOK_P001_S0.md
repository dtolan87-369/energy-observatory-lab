# Impossible Book — P001-S0 CAD Skeleton

## Goal

Prove digitally that:

- the book closes;
- every leaf turns;
- no normal parked mechanism envelope collides;
- each selected mechanism has a plausible home;
- hinge/root geometry and the state-reservation region can coexist.

## Candidate starting envelope

These are **not frozen standards**.

- page planform: about 100 × 140 mm
- cover planform: about 102 × 144 mm
- deep-leaf ceiling: about 16 mm

## First CAD hinge comparison

Carry two hinge/root concepts:

### A — segmented barrel + replaceable pin
Simple, stiff, conventional baseline.

### B — cartridge spine + conventional pin hinge
Cartridge handles removal/alignment; pin hinge handles rotation.

The CAD comparison chooses the first coupon candidate. It does not prove the final hinge.
